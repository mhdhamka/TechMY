## 1. Core Principles & Architecture
Before writing production code, establish the foundational mindset, structural design rules, and architectural thinking required by top-tier engineering organizations:

<div class="se-principles-grid">
  <div class="se-principle-card">
    <h3>Foundations & Quality</h3>
    <p>Master micro-level readability, rigorous code review cultures, automated testing standards, and long-term maintainability across large-scale systems.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="se-principle-card">
    <h3>Developer Mindset</h3>
    <p>Adopt an ownership mindset focused on DRY principles, orthogonality, automation discipline, and rapid tracer-bullet development.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="se-principle-card">
    <h3>Engineering Construction</h3>
    <p>Apply rigorous software construction practices, structural design rules, defensive coding safeguards, and table-driven logic methods.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="se-principle-card">
    <h3>Architectural Approach</h3>
    <p>Evaluate structural characteristics, balance architectural trade-offs, manage technical debt, and design scalable, reliable software systems.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## 2. Tech Stack Mastery
Move past basic syntax to write idiomatic, high-performance, secure, and type-safe code across your core technology stack:

<div class="testing-stack">
  <div class="testing-card se-card-ts">
    <div class="card-header">
      <h3>TypeScript</h3>
      <span class="tagline">React & Next.js</span>
    </div>
    <div class="card-body">
      <p><strong>Expectation:</strong> Leverage structural typing and type inference. Design types to make illegal states unrepresentable while completely eliminating loose `any` declarations.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="testing-card se-card-py">
    <div class="card-header">
      <h3>Python</h3>
      <span class="tagline">Backend & Automation</span>
    </div>
    <div class="card-body">
      <p><strong>Expectation:</strong> Implement custom dunder methods, apply gradual static typing via Mypy, and optimize repetitive loop execution using closures and `functools.cache`.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="testing-card se-card-cpp">
    <div class="card-header">
      <h3>C++</h3>
      <span class="tagline">Systems & Performance</span>
    </div>
    <div class="card-body">
      <p><strong>Expectation:</strong> Eliminate raw manual pointer allocations by utilizing smart pointers (`std::unique_ptr`, `std::shared_ptr`) and efficient move semantics.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="testing-card se-card-php">
    <div class="card-header">
      <h3>PHP & MySQL</h3>
      <span class="tagline">Enterprise Architecture</span>
    </div>
    <div class="card-body">
      <p><strong>Expectation:</strong> Adopt PSR-4 autoloading via Composer and secure all database queries against injection vulnerabilities using PDO prepared statements.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

## 3. Career, Leadership & Interview Prep
Preparing for competitive engineering loops and tech-lead progression requires balancing career ownership, architectural execution, and interviewing skills:

<div class="testing-stack">
  <div class="testing-card se-card-blue">
    <div class="card-header">
      <h3>The Engineering Resume</h3>
      <span class="tagline">Impact Formula</span>
    </div>
    <div class="card-body">
      <p><strong>Expectation:</strong> Learn to structure every bullet point using the strict formula: *Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]* while keeping your CV strictly to one page to pass ATS filters.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="testing-card se-card-green">
    <div class="card-header">
      <h3>Behavioral Matrix</h3>
      <span class="tagline">The 5-Story Grid</span>
    </div>
    <div class="card-body">
      <p><strong>Expectation:</strong> Prepare 5 to 6 versatile real-world stories using the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) to seamlessly handle prompts regarding leadership, failure, and technical trade-offs.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="testing-card se-card-orange">
    <div class="card-header">
      <h3>Algorithm Optimization</h3>
      <span class="tagline">The BUD Method</span>
    </div>
    <div class="card-body">
      <p><strong>Expectation:</strong> Master systematic problem-solving by identifying Bottlenecks, Unnecessary work, and Duplicate work before writing code, reinforced by DIY manual walkthroughs.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="testing-card se-card-blue">
    <div class="card-header">
      <h3>Tech Leadership & Growth</h3>
      <span class="tagline">Career & Execution</span>
    </div>
    <div class="card-body">
      <p><strong>Expectation:</strong> Own your career path, master performance reviews, manage projects, navigate team dynamics, and scale from senior engineer to principal and tech lead.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

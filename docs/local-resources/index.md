## 1. The Malaysian Tech Landscape: An Overview

Malaysia has evolved from an IT shared-services hub into a strategic regional powerhouse for high-concurrency FinTech, cloud data center corridors, and applied AI. With the establishment of the **Ministry of Digital**, the launch of the **National AI Office (NAIO)**, multi-billion dollar cloud investments from AWS (`ap-southeast-5`), Google Cloud, and Microsoft, and the **Cyber Security Act 2024**, local engineers operate in an increasingly sophisticated, globally connected ecosystem.

<div class="se-principles-grid">
  <div class="se-principle-card">
    <h3>1. Software Engineering & Scale-Ups</h3>
    <p>Home to homegrown unicorns and regional scale-ups (Grab's founding roots, Carsome, Aerodyne, PolicyStreet, StoreHub) building high-velocity consumer and enterprise applications.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="se-principle-card">
    <h3>2. System Design & FinTech Switch</h3>
    <p>World-class national payment rails (PayNet DuitNow, FPX) and five licensed digital banks (GXBank, Boost Bank, AEON Bank) demanding extreme ACID reliability and sub-second latency.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="se-principle-card">
    <h3>3. AI, Supercomputing & Local NLP</h3>
    <p>Hyperscale AI data centers in Johor and Cyberjaya, national AI frameworks under NAIO, and open-source breakthroughs in Bahasa Melayu LLMs led by communities like Mesolitica.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="se-principle-card">
    <h3>4. Sovereign Cloud & Cybersecurity</h3>
    <p>Strict regulatory oversight via Bank Negara Malaysia (BNM RMiT), National Cyber Security Agency (NACSA), and the updated Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA 2024).</p>
  </div>
</div>
